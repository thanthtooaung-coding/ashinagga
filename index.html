About

A life given to practice

Ashin Agga is a Theravāda Buddhist monk who ordained in Yangon, Myanmar. After years of study of the Pāḷi scriptures and intensive meditation practice, he now teaches students from many backgrounds — in person, at retreats, and online.

His teaching is simple and practical: steady mindfulness in daily life, generosity and kindness toward others, and seeing clearly the changing nature of all things. Students often say they value his patience, his warmth and his gift for explaining deep teachings in plain words that can be practised right away.
