Privacy Policy

Application: Pāli Keyboard
Package Name: com.ashinaggadhamma.palikeyboard
Developer: Ashin Agga Dhamma Darna
Last Updated: November 24, 2026

Core Privacy Commitment: Pāli Keyboard does not collect, record, transmit, or share any personal information, keystrokes, or sensitive data. The keyboard functions entirely offline on your device.

1. Information Collection and Use

Pāli Keyboard is an Input Method Editor (IME) designed for typing Pāli in Roman script (IAST) and Myanmar script. We believe in complete digital privacy:

  • No Keystroke Logging: We do not log, capture, or store what you type. All character input and transliteration conversions (including Velthuis combinations) take place strictly in temporary device memory.
  • No Personal Data: We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, location data, IP addresses, contacts, or device identifiers.
  • No Account Required: You do not need to create an account, log in, or provide any credentials to use this application.

2. Internet and Network Access

Pāli Keyboard does not declare or use the android.permission.INTERNET permission in its Android manifest. The application cannot connect to the internet, cloud servers, or any external service, ensuring that data typed into the keyboard cannot leave your physical device.

3. Device Permissions

The application only requests the minimum system permission required for keyboard usability:

  • android.permission.VIBRATE: Used exclusively to provide optional haptic touch feedback when a key is pressed. You can disable this feature in the app settings at any time.

4. Third-Party Services and Analytics

Pāli Keyboard does not incorporate any third-party SDKs, analytics frameworks, tracking libraries, or advertising networks. No user behavior or telemetry is monitored or shared with third parties.

5. Children’s Privacy

Because the app does not collect any personal data whatsoever, it is safe for users of all ages and does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date. Users are encouraged to review this page periodically.
